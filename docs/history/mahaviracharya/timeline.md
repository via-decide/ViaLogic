## Engineering summary
The chronological significance of Mahāvīrācārya is the movement from astronomy-centered computation toward a general mathematical handbook.

## Historical summary
The exact dates of Mahāvīrācārya's birth and death are not securely documented in the repository or the cited sources. Authoritative secondary histories commonly place him in the ninth century CE, with MacTutor giving approximately 800–870 and the MAA associating the treatise with that period.

## Chronological timeline
- **Before the ninth century:** Aryabhata and Brahmagupta establish influential Indian traditions in arithmetic, algebra, geometry, and astronomical computation.
- **c. 800 CE:** Probable beginning of Mahāvīrācārya's lifetime, according to MacTutor's approximate dating.
- **Ninth century:** Rashtrakuta-era scholarly culture supports Jain mathematical work in South India.
- **c. 850 CE:** *Gaṇita-sāra-saṅgraha* is commonly dated to this period; the work consolidates arithmetic and algebraic procedures into nine instructional chapters.
- **After composition:** The text circulates as a mathematical teaching source and influences later South Indian mathematical writing.
- **1912:** M. Rangacarya publishes an English translation, making the text accessible to modern historians of mathematics.
