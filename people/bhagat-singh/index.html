ACT I: NAVEEN BHARAT

The Young Firebrand

1907 — 1931 CE

Born in Banga (Punjab). Witness to the horrific Jallianwala Bagh massacre at age 12, he collected a bottle of the blood-soaked soil to remind him of the price of freedom.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He left home at 16 to join the HRA, refusing to get married as his life 'belonged to the nation.'
