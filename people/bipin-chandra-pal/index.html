ACT I: THE SWADESHI AWAKENING

The Prophet of Nationalism

1858 — 1932 CE

Born in Poil, Bengal. Bipin Chandra Pal was the third pillar of the Lal-Bal-Pal trio, a master orator and the architect of the Swadeshi movement's ideology.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He was one of the loudest voices against the partition of Bengal in 1905.
