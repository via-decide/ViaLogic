ACT I: THE STARRY MESSENGER

Galileo Galilei

1564 — 1642 CE

Born in Pisa, Italy. A giant of the Renaissance who turned his telescope to the heavens and proved that we live in a universe of motion, not a fixed, unchanging sphere.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He was the first to see the craters of the moon and the moons of Jupiter.
