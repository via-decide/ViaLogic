# Galileo Galilei

**Father of Modern Science**

- **Era:** 1564–1642
- **Origin:** Pisa, Italy
- **Field:** Astronomy, Physics

Galileo improved the telescope, discovered the moons of Jupiter, and stood firm on the heliocentric theory despite the Inquisition. He pioneered the experimental scientific method.
