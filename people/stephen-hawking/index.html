Stephen Hawking

1942 — 2018 CE

Born in Oxford, England. Despite a life-altering disability, he traveled to the edge of black holes and the beginning of time using only the power of his mind.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He held the Lucasian Chair of Mathematics at Cambridge, once held by Isaac Newton.
