ACT I: DEFYING THE DOCTRINE

The Unbowed Queen

1778 — 1829 CE

Born in Kakati, Karnataka. Rani Chennamma was the first female ruler to lead an armed rebellion against the British East India Company, decades before the 1857 uprising.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: She defied the 'Doctrine of Lapse' 30 years before Rani Laxmibai.
