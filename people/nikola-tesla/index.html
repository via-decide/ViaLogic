ACT I: THE WARDENCLYFFE TOWER

Nikola Tesla

1856 — 1943 CE

Born in Smiljan, Croatia. An inventor, electrical engineer, and futurist who dreamt of a world powered by free, wireless energy and the invisible forces of the lightning bolt.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He held over 300 patents, including the fundamental design for AC power.
