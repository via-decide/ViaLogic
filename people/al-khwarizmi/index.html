ACT I: THE HOUSE OF WISDOM

The Scholar of Khwarazm

c. 780 CE — Baghdad

Born in Khwarazm (modern Uzbekistan), c. 780 CE. He joined the House of Wisdom in Baghdad under Caliph al-Ma'mun and became its greatest scholar.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He compiled, translated and synthesized Greek, Indian, and Persian knowledge into Arabic.
