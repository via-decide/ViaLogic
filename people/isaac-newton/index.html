ACT I: THE PHILOSOPHIÆ NATURALIS

Isaac Newton

1643 — 1727 CE

Born in Woolsthorpe, England. The man who decoded the hidden mechanics of the world, unifying the apple falling from the tree with the planets orbiting the sun.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He invented Calculus (simultaneously with Leibniz) to describe the change in motion.
