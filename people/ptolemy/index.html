ACT I: THE ALMAGEST

The Greek of Egypt

c. 100 — 170 CE

Claudius Ptolemy lived in Alexandria during the height of the Roman Empire. He was a mathematician, astronomer, and geographer of legendary influence.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: His Almagest remained the undisputed authority on astronomy for over 1,400 years.
