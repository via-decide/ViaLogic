ACT I: THE BRAHMASPHUTASIDDHANTA

The Scholar of Ujjain

598 — 668 CE

Born 598 CE in Bhinmal, Rajasthan. Became head of the astronomical observatory at Ujjain — the mathematical capital of ancient India.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: Ujjain sits on India's ancient prime meridian — the Greenwich of antiquity.
