ACT I: POLONIUM AND RADIUM

Marie Curie

1867 — 1934 CE

Born Maria Skłodowska in Warsaw, Poland. A pioneer who sacrificed her health to discover the glowing power of radioactive elements, forever changing medicine and physics.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: She is the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields: Physics and Chemistry.
