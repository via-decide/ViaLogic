ACT I: NERO'S ADVISOR

Seneca the Younger

4 BCE — 65 CE

Born in Cordoba, Spain. A Roman statesman, dramatist, and the most eloquent voice of Stoicism, who balanced a life of immense wealth and political danger with a philosophy of radical inner calm.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He was the tutor and later the advisor to the notorious Emperor Nero.
