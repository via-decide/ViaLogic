ACT I: THE RITUAL OF RESISTANCE

Manyam Veerudu

1897 — 1924 CE

Born in Pandrangi (Andhra Pradesh). A sanyasi who turned revolutionary when he saw the British forest laws destroying the lives of his tribal brothers in the Manyam region.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He was called 'Manyam Veerudu' (Hero of the Jungle) by the local tribes.
