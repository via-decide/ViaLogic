ACT I: THE SIMON COMMISSION

Sher-e-Punjab

1865 — 1928 CE

Born in Dhudike, Punjab. Lala Lajpat Rai was a pillar of the 'Extremist' faction of the Congress, a man whose thunderous voice earned him the title 'The Lion of Punjab.'

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He was one third of the famous Lal-Bal-Pal trio.
