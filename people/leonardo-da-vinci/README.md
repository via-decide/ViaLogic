# Leonardo da Vinci

**The Universal Genius**

- **Era:** 1452–1519
- **Origin:** Vinci, Italy
- **Field:** Polymathy, Anatomy, Engineering

The quintessential Renaissance man, Leonardo excelled at everything from painting the Mona Lisa to conceptualizing flying machines and automated knights. His notebooks contain the seeds of modern anatomy and hydraulics.
