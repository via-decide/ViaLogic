ACT I: KAKORI BRAVERY

The Warrior Poet

1900 — 1927 CE

Born in Shahjahanpur (Uttar Pradesh). A beautiful soul who wrote poetry under the pen names 'Hasrat' and 'Warsi,' he became one of the key leaders of the Hindustan Republican Association.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He and Ram Prasad Bismil were inseparable friends, a symbol of communal harmony.
