ACT I: NANA SAHEB'S GENERAL

The Shadow General

1814 — 1859 CE

Born Ramachandra Pandurang Tope in Maharashtra. He was the brilliant military heart of the 1857 rebellion, serving as the right hand of Nana Saheb Peshwa.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: His name 'Tantia' means 'Commander' in local dialect.
