The Rebel Queen

1828 — 1858 CE

Born Manikarnika in Varanasi. She was trained in horse riding, sword fighting, and archery — skills that were unconventional for women of her time.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: She became the Rani of Jhansi after marrying Raja Gangadhar Rao.
