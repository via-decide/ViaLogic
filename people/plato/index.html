ACT I: THE ALLEGORY OF THE CAVE

Plato

428 — 348 BCE

Socrates' greatest student and the founder of the Academy. He believed that the physical world we see is only a shadow of a higher, more perfect reality.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He founded the Academy in Athens, the first institution of higher learning in the Western world.
