ACT I: THE KAKORI INCIDENT

The Unstoppable

1906 — 1931 CE

Born in Bhabhra (Madhya Pradesh). At age 15, when arrested during the Non-Cooperation Movement, he told the magistrate his name was 'Azad' (Free), his father's name 'Swatantrata' (Independence), and his home was 'Jail.'

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He famously vowed that the British would never capture him alive.
