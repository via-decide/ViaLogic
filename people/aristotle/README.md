# Aristotle

**The Master of Those Who Know**

| | |
|---|---|
| Era | 384–322 BC |
| Origin | Stagira, Greece |
| Field | Logic, Ethics, Science |

Aristotle created formal logic, classified the sciences, and wrote on everything from biology to poetry. His works dominated Western thought for nearly two millennia and remain foundational today.
