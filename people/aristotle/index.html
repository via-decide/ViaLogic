ACT I: THE LYCEUM

Aristotle

384 — 322 BCE

Born in Stagira, Greece. Plato's brightest student and the tutor of Alexander the Great. He grounded philosophy in the physical world, creating the first systematic classification of knowledge.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He founded the Lyceum, where he and his students would walk while debating (Peripatetics).
