ACT I: THE DAUGHTER OF THEON

The Perfect Human Being

c. 360 CE — Alexandria

Born c. 360 CE in Alexandria, daughter of mathematician Theon. He trained her to be "a perfect human being." She surpassed her teacher and became head of the Neoplatonist school.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: She was a beacon of knowledge in an era of growing religious turbulence.
