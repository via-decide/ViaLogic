# John Locke

**The Father of Liberalism**

| | |
|---|---|
| Era | 1632–1704 |
| Origin | Somerset, England |
| Field | Empiricism & Politics |

Locke argued the mind begins as a blank slate (tabula rasa) and that legitimate government rests on the consent of the governed. His ideas directly inspired the American Revolution.
