ACT I: MEDITATIONS IN THE TENT

Marcus Aurelius

121 — 180 CE

The last of the 'Five Good Emperors' of Rome. He ruled the world's most powerful empire while maintaining a secret journal of stoic wisdom, written in military tents during his campaigns.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: His 'Meditations' was never intended for publication; it was his private diary for self-improvement.
