ACT I: THE AGORA DEBATES

Socrates

470 — 399 BCE

Born in Athens, Greece. The father of Western philosophy, who never wrote a single page, but whose relentless questioning exposed the ignorance of the powerful.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He served as a hoplite soldier in the Peloponnesian War before becoming a philosopher.
