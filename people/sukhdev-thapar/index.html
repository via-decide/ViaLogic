The Silent Strategist

1907 — 1931 CE

Born in Ludhiana, Punjab. Sukhdev was the intellectual brain and the recruitment mastermind behind the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA).

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He was the chief of the Punjab unit of the HSRA and a master organizer.
