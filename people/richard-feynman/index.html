ACT I: QUANTUM ELECTRODYNAMICS

Richard Feynman

1918 — 1988 CE

Born in New York, USA. A Nobel-winning physicist, bongo player, and safecracker who transformed complex mathematics into simple, visual 'Feynman Diagrams.'

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He helped develop the atomic bomb at Los Alamos while still in his 20s.
