# David Hume

**The Great Sceptic**

| | |
|---|---|
| Era | 1711–1776 |
| Origin | Edinburgh, Scotland |
| Field | Empiricism & Skepticism |

Hume demolished the notion of causation as anything more than habit and argued that reason is the slave of the passions. He awakened Kant from his "dogmatic slumber."
