ACT I: THE SWARAJYA DREAM

The Born King

1630 — 1680 CE

Born in Shivneri Fort to Shahaji and Jijabai. In an era of Mughal and Adilshahi dominance, he dared to dream of 'Hindavi Swarajya' — self-rule for the people.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He took the oath of Swarajya at the age of 15 at Raireshwar temple.
