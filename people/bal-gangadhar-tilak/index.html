ACT I: SWARAJ IS MY BIRTHRIGHT

Lokmanya Tilak

1856 — 1920 CE

Born in Ratnagiri, Maharashtra. Known as 'The Father of Indian Unrest,' he was the first leader to transform the independence struggle into a mass movement.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He gave the famous slogan: 'Swaraj is my birthright, and I shall have it!'
