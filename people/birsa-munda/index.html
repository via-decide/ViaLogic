ACT I: THE ULGULAN

Dharati Aaba

1875 — 1900 CE

Born in Ulihatu (Jharkhand). A tribal leader of the Munda community, he stood up against the British exploitation and the destruction of the tribal way of life.

HISTORICAL INSIGHT: He was called 'Dharati Aaba' or the Father of the Earth by his people.
